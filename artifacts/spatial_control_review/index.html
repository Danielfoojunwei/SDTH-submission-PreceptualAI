What this evidence supports

A finite native simulation diagnosticFour seeds in one warehouse family, not four independent physical warehouses. Three carriers: 2.4 / 5.2 / 6.5 GHz; 6.5 GHz extrapolates training. No physical-device calibration; interference disabled.

A control-stack result, not a JEPA win48 fresh simulations; 2 additional disabled-parity runs. Frozen regression, zero new neural training fits. Support is an AP-neighbor heuristic, not a packet-risk model. The causal command kernel differs from the earlier native diagnostic.

No default or deployment promotionThe 4 s failures are preserved. SERVICE remains unopened; no physical-car actions. No JEPA-objective, SOTA, multi-site generalization or production-reliability claim follows from these four episodes.
