04 / What the residual actually carries

Inspect the gate and its scale together.

A small gate can be offset by a larger learned prior. These diagnostics retain their combined effect and separate fixed, learned and effective feature variation.

Feature scale α

Learned per member, fixed at inference and shared across APs/queries; not an online AP veto. Projected finite parameters lie in [0,1]. Boundary behavior and exact zero do not guarantee useful reactivation or better held-out error.

Effective coefficient-prior RMS

dB residual units before conditioning and precision jitter. This is neither predictive deviation, a uniform uncertainty floor, an identified material nor packet risk.

Function inclusion, not a guarantee

Zero design recovers a same-weight global-eight function up to roundoff. It need not match independently trained baseline weights, and adding the option does not guarantee noninferiority.

Static prediction, not service

This phase has zero new warehouse simulations, physical actions or learned temporal transitions. Field error cannot qualify 100-car packet reliability or arbitrary protocols.
