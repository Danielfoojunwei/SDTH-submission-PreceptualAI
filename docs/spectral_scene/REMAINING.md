# RadioJEPA: remaining work and evidence boundaries

Evidence snapshot: **9 November 2026**, during construction of the independent spectral-field study. This is a gap matrix for [the archived user plan](../plans/RADIOJEPA_USER_PLAN.md), not a claim that the full program is finished. This review read source and retained receipts; it ran no training, collection, model inference or confirmation experiment.

RadioMap remains the application default and fallback. Existing WirelessJEV mission compilation, radio heuristics, packet simulation, trust gates and execution tooling are legacy capabilities. Their existence does not qualify a new RadioJEPA world model. The separate observation-frontier campaign remains unlaunched, as stated in [the execution scope](../RADIOJEPA_EXECUTION.md).

## Evidence levels

| Level | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Implemented | A source path/API exists and rejects unsupported inputs where specified. | Successful fitting, useful representations, transfer or service improvement. |
| Unit verified | A retained test receipt/analytic fixture checks the stated contract. | Accuracy on a scientific holdout, physical hardware capability or production eligibility. |
| Trained and evaluated | Independent fit receipts, predictions and declared endpoint metrics exist. | A different modality/task, an untested action, universal device transfer or safety. |
| Qualified | The specific head/controller has passed the independent promotion contract with its required rights, service, tail, compute and hardware evidence. | Qualification of other heads or environments. **No new RadioJEPA head has this status.** |

The completed pilot's [manifest](../../artifacts/radiojepa/verification/manifest.json) binds **452 source files and 864 evidence files**. Its retained [preflight](../../artifacts/radiojepa/preflight/tests.json) reports exit code zero; the bound stdout records **2,041 passed**. Its [passive audit](../../artifacts/radiojepa/verification/raw_audit.json) records 18 fits and 54 paired prediction streams with `passes=true`. These are retained records, not checks rerun by this document. Do not edit those sources, documents or evidence to retrofit the new study.

The measured-view pilot trained six cells with three independent members each. Its best reported mean factory RF RMSE is **2.650 ± 0.135 dB** for `joint_vicreg`. At the same SIGReg setting, `joint_sigreg` reports **2.908 ± 0.329 dB**, versus **2.732 ± 0.203 dB** for matched regression. Comparing the best VICReg cell with the SIGReg regression cell does not isolate the JEPA objective. These are referenced array-channel powers, not dBm, packet loss, SmartCar localization qualification or SOTA. See [the pilot results](../RADIOJEPA_RESULTS.md) and [raw report](../../artifacts/radiojepa/report.json).

At this snapshot, source/protocol/registry and test fixtures are being completed; no new scientific fit/result receipt is evidence yet. | Isolated static RF-field prediction in two generated CAD families. **Not 24 measured warehouses, 100-robot service confirmation, interference/MAC validation or hardware testing.** |

The new [protocol](../../artifacts/radiojepa_spectral/protocol.json), [execution order](../../artifacts/radiojepa_spectral/execution_order.json) and [plan](PLAN.md) govern the field study. The executing agent reports **19 data/operator tests passed in 2.20 s**; the final source-bound full preflight is still pending completion of all new files. Subset checks and authored `test_spectral_*.py` fixtures are not that final preflight or a scientific result. Registration, publication, collection and fitting must be established by their own receipts. The original service holdouts must not be opened by a FIELD-test permission.

The RT solver has a finite path budget. A link with **no solved path is unresolved/missing**, not a verified weak or censored RF measurement. RF RMSE and absolute-error percentiles condition on **heard reported readings**; every result must retain heard, censored and missing counts rather than imply full-link/NLOS coverage. Main in-band queries predict the six **odd, spatially withheld poses**. The separate 6 GHz assay queries the **even context poses on the same physical links**, isolating conditional frequency transport; it does not also establish 6 GHz prediction at unseen positions. Neither assay supplies interference, MAC, queue or application-service evidence.

The hierarchy schemas and conformal/barrier ideas are not a license to claim a guaranteed fallback, a universal radio foundation model, arbitrary protocol/device support, or “JEPA beats RadioMap.” Each narrower endpoint needs its own evidence. The archived plan's bracketed paper numbers remain hypotheses.

## Next executable steps and stopping rules

1. **Finish the new source proof.** Complete the spectral model/training/baseline/study tests, run the final preflight on exactly the source to be registered, verify the old closure unchanged, and publish the new protocol, 84 specifications, execution order and source capsule. The current code makes publication a prerequisite for science. A registry is a list of specifications, not collected fields.
2. **Run only the registered field sequence.** Collect 48 TRAIN and eight VAL scenes; fit training-only front-end/numerical priors; train nine initial cells; select the prior on VAL; fit six matched controls; complete the numerical references. Checksum every measurement/checkpoint/prediction and preserve missing/unresolved links. Source changes after registration require an explicit separate amendment, never silent retrospective editing.
3. **Evaluate the four DEV geometries once.** Apply the declared finite-output, RF RMSE/p95, every-seed and zero-latent-use gate. With four scenes a centered invariant scene-code matrix has rank at most three; rank cannot be a fabricated “above 7/48” success criterion. If the gate fails, retain the negative result and veto. Do not tune on these scenes until a win appears or open FIELD tests anyway.
4. **Open the new 24 FIELD tests only if that gate passes.** Keep the original SERVICE holdouts closed. Preserve the four declared contrasts, 98.75% paired whole-geometry intervals, main source-view-A information budget, individual-member scoring and no prediction ensembling. Report same-link held-band transport separately from odd-pose spatial prediction, matched-regression differences, censor likelihood, coverage/missing counts, zero/shuffled-latent sensitivity, timing and descriptive device onboarding. A gain against RadioMap without a gain against matched regression does not establish JEPA-objective necessity.
5. **Close the field experiment honestly.** Passively verify raw receipts and predictions; report nulls/harm, hidden assumptions and coverage. A better heard-RSSI RMSE/p95 is a field-prediction result. Fixed likelihood spread, latent rank and lower reconstruction loss do not qualify uncertainty or service. Refresh this snapshot's pending statuses only from the resulting receipts.
6. **Bridge to service before promotion.** Build a supported learned RF-to-service head/adapter, delivered-observation filter and action-conditioned dynamics. Freeze a separate same-planner/same-gate packet experiment with paid sensing, capture, transport, scene construction and query compute. Include strong RadioMap and matched supervised controls, complete command exposure and service/tail endpoints. Reserve/open SERVICE confirmation only under its independent authorization; the FIELD permission never supplies it.
7. **Then extend modality/device/scale.** Run eligible real RSSI/FTM onboarding, complex-CSI/path and adequately aligned camera experiments as separate protocols; fit scaling curves across actual scene/view/device/model budgets. Keep simulated devices, array receivers, procedural CAD families and physical warehouses distinct in every count.

These steps are already within the research scope. Public acquisition and reversible implementation do not require another permission question. An actual missing credential, unavailable licensed asset or physical access route is a concrete blocker to report; it is not grounds to invent data or a generic request to approve more research.
