# Spatial support and client-local recovery: fixed diagnostic

This new study addresses concrete failures in the completed native bridge.
It does not modify that study, train a replacement model or open the original
SERVICE confirmation layouts. The registered JSON is authoritative for exact
membership, clocks, limits, order and costs.

Four fresh procedural episode seeds, 100 robots and 20 seconds are evaluated
separately with 0.03 s and 4 s handovers. Six arms cross support/recovery for
the fixed validation-selected regression checkpoint, plus RadioMap with and
without the same local recovery. There are 48 fresh simulations and two
disabled-extension wiring-parity simulations. All outcomes are retained;
no seed, threshold or architecture is selected from them. No additional fit
or claim of JEPA-objective superiority is part of this diagnostic.

The new kernel requires a central command to be created and carried by a
subsequently created, actually delivered control frame before either handover
cost can execute. A 100 ms planner allowance delays command creation, with
late computations discarded; pending commands expire after one second and
same-target proposals do not renew them. The packet reservation is the
existing 128 bytes, with no extra successful transport inferred. This is a
changed execution model, shared by all fresh arms, so its results cannot be
compared as a one-variable intervention against the old native diagnostic.

The support guard requires the latest own measured report on at least one
source band (2.4 or 5.2 GHz) to be heard, at least −80 dBm, no more than six
seconds old and within five metres of the public query position. A newer
censor bound blocks fallback to an older positive reading on that band. It
may pass on another actually measured source band. This is AP-neighbor
evidence, **not** calibration of 6.5 GHz, every band or packet risk. Additional
delivered-history information and its indexing cost are explicit. The
encoder and its 64-token selection stay unchanged. Memory construction is
charged inside forecast completion. At admission, payload-matched server
deliveries received while the forecast queued refresh the guard's evidence;
local-only undelivered scans remain unavailable to it. This refresh and all
admission work are included in the paid central planner allowance.

Recovery is a separate simulated preinstalled capability. Each client sees
only its own completed paid scans, current valid pilots and past delivery
status. A 200 ms silence trigger, five-second scan-age limit, −75 dBm target
floor, 8 dB margin and five-second dwell/backoff are engineering heuristics.
The margin applies when a fresh current-link measurement exists; otherwise
only the absolute target floor is available, without an invented current
RSSI or switching gain.
Autonomous scans cost 90 ms and share the same per-client five-second minimum
start spacing with background scans. A 10 ms local decision delay is charged;
work exceeding five milliseconds is rejected. Local handovers charge the full
declared interruption and cancel stale central pending actions. No reachable
good link or safety guarantee is assumed. Warm scan bins do not establish
exact scan-completion clocks and are not used as local recovery payloads.
Overrun work also blocks subsequent local ticks until its measured completion;
delayed execution revalidates its evidence within the remaining decision
allowance and discards late validation. These synchronous accounting rules
are not a physical hard-real-time guarantee.

Sensing capability and cadence limits are shared; realized observations and
outages can differ as consequences of local recovery. The native fading,
blockage, contention, retries, measurement noise, missing captures and top-K
censoring remain. Collision/interference additions stay off, as in the prior
diagnostic. Native 6.5 GHz, extruded CAD, zero receiver bias and PHY table
approximations remain unqualified simulator assumptions.
Lagged pilots require the same actual association and an available receiver
at capture and use time. Between valid captures the shared native PHY keeps
its last control estimate; that held estimate is not a new local observation.

Source, protocol, unit-test proof, prior seals and the fixed checkpoint are
bound before execution and remotely observed before any fresh world or
forward. Once-only attempt files prevent replacement of partial runs. Raw
packet counts, gaps, command/scan/action ledgers, forecasts and observable
history are archived for passive independent review. Every episode and arm,
mean contrast and harm count are reported per handover cost. Reports also
retain end-of-episode pending command packets separately, alongside the
conservative observed-miss-plus-pending fraction. The main observed counts
do not label an unfinished packet a measured success. Four episodes
from one warehouse family support a descriptive diagnostic, not a deployment,
SOTA, generalization or rare-event SLA claim. RadioMap stays the default.
